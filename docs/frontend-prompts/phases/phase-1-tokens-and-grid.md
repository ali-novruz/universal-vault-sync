### 3. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS & RULES

1. **8-Point Spatial Rhythm & CSS Variables:**
   - Define exact spacing tokens: `--space-2xs: 4px; --space-xs: 8px; --space-sm: 12px; --space-md: 16px; --space-lg: 24px; --space-xl: 32px; --space-2xl: 48px; --space-3xl: 64px; --space-4xl: 96px;`.
   - Implement fluid container widths: `--container-max: 1440px; --container-narrow: 1100px;`.

2. **Perceptual OKLCH / HSL Color Architecture:**
   - **Deep Substrates:** `--bg-base: #08090d; --bg-surface: #0f121a; --bg-card: #151a26; --bg-glass: rgba(15, 18, 26, 0.75);`
   - **Luminous Synaptic Cyan:** `--cyan-sync: #00f0ff; --cyan-glow: rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.35); --cyan-dim: #008fa0;`
   - **Knowledge Vault Amethyst:** `--purple-vault: #a855f7; --purple-glow: rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.35);`
   - **Deterministic Emerald:** `--green-verified: #10b981; --green-glow: rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35);`
   - **Agent Hue Matrix:**
     - `--agent-claude: #d97706; --agent-antigravity: #3b82f6; --agent-cursor: #f43f5e; --agent-windsurf: #14b8a6; --agent-aider: #eab308; --agent-cline: #8b5cf6; --agent-copilot: #64748b;`
   - **Lines & Borders:** `--line-subtle: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08); --line-focus: rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4);`

3. **Fluid Typography Scales (`clamp()`):**
   - Headings: `font-family: 'Syne', sans-serif;` with letter-spacing tracking (`-0.03em`).
     - Display Hero: `font-size: clamp(2.8rem, 6.5vw, 5.2rem); line-height: 0.95;`
     - Section Title: `font-size: clamp(1.8rem, 3.5vw, 2.6rem); line-height: 1.1;`
   - Technical Monospace: `font-family: 'JetBrains Mono', 'Space Mono', monospace; font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;`
     - Telemetry & Headers: `font-size: clamp(0.72rem, 1.2vw, 0.85rem); letter-spacing: 0.15em; text-transform: uppercase;`
   - Body & Prose: `font-family: 'Inter', 'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif; font-size: clamp(0.95rem, 1.1vw, 1.1rem); line-height: 1.65;`

4. **Ergonomic Layouts & Touch Targets:**
   - Full dynamic viewport support (`min-height: 100dvh;`).
   - Mobile touch targets conforming to Hoober thumb-zone guidelines (minimum `44x44px` clickable hitboxes).
   - Multi-layer glassmorphism: `backdrop-filter: blur(24px); -webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(24px);`.

### 4. DELIVERABLES
